Privacy Policy: Crypto Mining Tycoon

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Mortek ("we", "us", "our") operates the Crypto Mining Tycoon mobile application (the "App"). This page explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

We do not build user profiles, and we do not sell personal data. The only server we operate is the optional support chat described under "Support Chat" below.

Information We Collect

  • Game progress. Your save data is stored locally on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games Services, a copy is also stored in Google's cloud save so your progress survives a reinstall or a new device.
  • Advertising identifiers and device data. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID together with technical device and usage data. See "Advertising" below.
  • Google Play Games profile data. If you sign in, Google Play Games Services may process your player name, profile picture, and game scores for leaderboards.
  • Support chat messages you choose to send. The App has an optional support chat in Settings. Only if you send a message, the message text, an optional email address you choose to enter, the app's language setting, app version, platform and a random install identifier (not linked to your identity) are sent to our support server. See "Support Chat" below.

Advertising

The App is free and is funded by optional rewarded video ads, which you only ever see after choosing to watch one. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob.

To serve, measure, and report on ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system, and information about how you interact with ads. This happens whether ads are personalized or not, though less data is used when they are not personalized.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's certified consent management platform. You may accept or decline, and declining does not restrict any part of the game: you will simply receive non-personalized ads instead.

You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad Privacy → Manage.

You can also limit ad personalization at the device level through your Android settings, under Google, Ads. For more detail, see how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. These transactions are processed entirely by Google. We do not receive or store your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles payment data.

Google Play Games Services

Signing in with a Google account is optional and enables leaderboards and cloud saves. If you do not sign in, the App remains fully playable and your progress is kept locally on your device. Data handled through Play Games Services is processed by Google under its Privacy Policy, and we have no direct access to your Google account.

Local Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may send reminders such as offline earnings being ready. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device, and no data is sent anywhere to produce them.

Support Chat

The optional support chat sends your message, an optional email address you enter, the app's language setting, app version, platform and a random install identifier to our support server, which runs on Cloudflare. A hashed form of your IP address is used only to limit abuse. Each new message also triggers a notification email to us, sent through Scaleway (Transactional Email, France), so the message text and the email address you may have entered pass through that service. Scaleway acts only as our processor. We use this data only to answer you and do not share or sell it. You can delete your conversation at any time in the App (Settings, Support), which removes it from our server, or ask us to delete it by email. Deleting a conversation removes the stored thread and all its messages from our server. It does not automatically remove the notification emails already sent to the developer mailbox through Scaleway, or the rate limit records, which hold only a hashed form of your IP address and a counter, no message content, in one-hour windows; an old record is cleared only when the same address or install next contacts the support server, so it can remain until then. Email us to have the notification emails deleted. Data is encrypted in transit.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob, for rewarded advertising. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing, for in-app purchases. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services, for leaderboards and cloud saves. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our support chat server, used only if you send a support message. Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Scaleway (France), whose Transactional Email service sends us a notification email when you send a support message. Scaleway acts only as our processor.

Data Retention

Local save data stays on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Cloud saves are retained by Google Play Games Services until you remove them through your Google account. Support conversations are stored on our server until you delete them in the App or ask us to. See "Requesting Data Deletion" below for how to have them removed sooner.

Requesting Data Deletion

This section applies to the Crypto Mining Tycoon app, published by Mortek.

How to request deletion. In the App, open Settings, Support and delete the conversation, which removes it from our server at once. Or send an email to mauricemoret1991@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request". Include the email address you entered in the support chat, if any, so that we can locate your conversation. There is no account to sign in to and no form to fill in. We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we delete. Your support conversation: the messages, the email address you may have entered, and the random install identifier. Versions of the App before 4.9.1 also had a feedback form; a submission made through it (name, email address and message) is deleted on request as well. This is all the data we hold directly. Notification emails about your messages that were already sent to our developer mailbox through Scaleway, and the counts-only rate limit records described under "Support Chat", are not removed automatically; on request we delete the emails by hand.

What we cannot delete for you, and where to go instead:

  • Game progress stored on your device is never sent to us. Remove it by uninstalling the App, or by clearing the App's data in your Android settings.
  • Cloud saves, leaderboard scores, and your player profile are held by Google Play Games Services. Remove these through your Google account.
  • Advertising data such as your advertising ID and approximate location is held by Google and its advertising partners, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings under Google, Ads, and you can withdraw advertising consent in the App under Settings → Ad Privacy → Manage.

Retention after a request. Once a deletion request is completed we keep no copy of your conversation or feedback submission on our support server. We may retain a minimal record of the request itself where we are required to demonstrate that we acted on it.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data relating to you, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent you previously gave.

For advertising consent, the quickest route is Settings → Ad Privacy → Manage inside the App. For data held by Google through Play Games Services, Play Billing, or AdMob, use your Google account controls. For anything we hold directly, such as a support conversation, delete it in the App or contact us using the email below and we will respond.
