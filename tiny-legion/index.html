Privacy Policy: Tiny Legion: Idle Horde

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Tiny Legion: Idle Horde (package com.mortek.tinylegion) collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

We do not sell any personal data, and we collect none except what you send in the optional support chat (see below). The game has no account and no login. Your progress is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the game. Your save may be included in your device's Google backup if you have it enabled.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data. This collection is done by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Where required (for example in the EU, UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the game's Settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases, such as gems, packs and Remove Ads, are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which products you own and a purchase reference from Google Play, so it can unlock them and avoid crediting a purchase twice. These are stored only on your device, in the game's save file.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you on this device, for example when your idle stores are full, a chest is ready, a daily reward is ready or a boost has ended. These reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device; no data is sent anywhere to produce them. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your Android settings.

Backups and transfer code

The game keeps a few daily backup copies of your save on your device and can show a transfer code that contains your save, so you can move your progress to another device. The backups and the code are created and read on your device; they are not sent to our servers. Anyone who has your transfer code can restore your progress, so keep it private.

Rating prompt

The game can ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review. This is handled by Google Play; we do not receive your review or any data from it.

Links to other games

The game has a "More games" card in Settings. Tapping it opens a web page in your browser. The game itself sends no data when showing or opening it.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Support chat

The game has an optional support chat in Settings. Only if you send a message, the message text, an optional email address you choose to enter, your language, app version, platform and a random install identifier (not linked to your identity) are sent to our support server, which runs on Cloudflare. A hashed form of your IP address is used only to limit abuse. We use this data only to answer you. We do not share or sell it. You can delete your conversation at any time in the app (Settings, Support), which removes it from our server, or ask us to delete it by email.

Data security and deletion

Data handled by Google services is encrypted in transit. Local game data, including backups, is removed when you uninstall the game. Support conversations are stored on our server until you delete them in the app or ask us to; data is encrypted in transit. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or Switzerland), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we hold is what you send in the support chat; to use these rights, delete the conversation in the app or contact us by email. Data collected by Google for ads is controlled by Google and your Android and Google account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
