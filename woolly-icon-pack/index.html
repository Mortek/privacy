Privacy Policy: Woolly: Knitted Wool Icons

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android icon pack Woolly: Knitted Wool Icons (package com.mortek.icons.woolly) collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

We do not sell any personal data, and we collect none except what you choose to send with an icon request (see below). The app has no account and no login. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. Google Play processes the purchase of the app under its own privacy policy.

Installed apps list

To show which of your apps still have no icon in this pack, the app reads the list of launchable apps installed on your device. This list stays on your device until you tap Send on the Request screen.

Icon requests

Only when you tap Send on the Request screen, the app sends the package names of the apps you see and ticked (at most 150), the pack name, the app version, your phone language, a random install identifier (not linked to your identity) and an optional email address that you type yourself to our support server, which runs on Cloudflare. A hashed form of your IP address is used only to limit abuse. Each new request also triggers a notification email to us, sent through Scaleway (Transactional Email, France), so the request text and the email address you may have entered pass through that service. Scaleway acts only as our processor. We use this data only to add icons and to answer you. We do not share or sell it. Data is encrypted in transit. Nothing is sent automatically. The Share button only opens the Android share sheet and sends nothing to us.

Permissions

The only permission is INTERNET, used when you tap Send on an icon request.

Deletion

To have your request and email address deleted, write to the address below. Deleting a request removes the stored thread from our server. It does not automatically remove the notification emails already sent to the developer mailbox, or the rate limit records, which hold only a hashed form of your IP address and a counter. Email us to have the notification emails deleted. Everything else (the pack itself) is removed from your device when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or Switzerland), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we hold is what you send with an icon request; to use these rights, contact us by email.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
