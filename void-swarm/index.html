Privacy Policy: Void Swarm: Idle Survivor

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Void Swarm: Idle Survivor (package com.mortek.voidswarm) collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data ourselves. The game has no account, no login and no server of its own. Your progress is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data. This collection is done by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Where required (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which products you own, so it can unlock them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Data security and deletion

Data handled by Google services is encrypted in transit. Because we store no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete; uninstalling the game removes all local game data. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
