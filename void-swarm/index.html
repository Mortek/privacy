Privacy Policy: Void Swarm: Idle Survivor

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Void Swarm: Idle Survivor (package com.mortek.voidswarm) collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

We do not sell any personal data, and we collect none except what you send in the optional support chat (see below). The game has no account and no login. Your progress is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the game. Your save may be included in your device's Google backup if you have it enabled.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data. This collection is done by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Where required (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the game's Settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which products you own and a purchase reference from Google Play, so it can unlock them and avoid crediting a purchase twice. These are stored only on your device, in the game's save file.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Support chat

The game has an optional support chat in Settings. Only if you send a message, the message text, an optional email address you choose to enter, your language, app version, platform and a random install identifier (not linked to your identity) are sent to our support server, which runs on Cloudflare. A hashed form of your IP address is used only to limit abuse. We use this data only to answer you. We do not share or sell it. You can delete your conversation at any time in the app (Settings, Support), which removes it from our server, or ask us to delete it by email.

Data security and deletion

Data handled by Google services is encrypted in transit. Local game data is removed when you uninstall the game. Support conversations are stored on our server until you delete them in the app or ask us to; data is encrypted in transit. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
