Privacy Policy: Neon: Glowing Tube Icons

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android icon pack Neon: Glowing Tube Icons (package com.mortek.icons.neon) collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

We do not sell any personal data, and we collect none except what you choose to send with an icon request (see below). The app has no account and no login. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. Google Play processes the purchase of the app under its own privacy policy.

Installed apps list

To show which of your apps still have no icon in this pack, the app reads the list of launchable apps installed on your device. This list stays on your device until you tap Send on the Request screen.

Icon requests

Only when you tap Send on the Request screen, the app sends the package names of the apps you see and ticked (at most 150), the pack name, the app version, your phone language, a random install identifier (not linked to your identity) and an optional email address that you type yourself to our support server, which runs on Cloudflare. A hashed form of your IP address is used only to limit abuse. Each new request also triggers a notification email to us, sent through Scaleway (Transactional Email, France), so the request text and the email address you may have entered pass through that service. Scaleway acts only as our processor. We use this data only to add icons and to answer you. We do not share or sell it. Data is encrypted in transit. Nothing is sent when you only browse, search or apply icons. The Share button only opens the Android share sheet and sends nothing to us.

Messages to the developer

If you use "Message the developer" on the About screen and tap Send, the text you write, an optional email address you type, a random installation identifier, the app version and your phone language are sent to our support server (Cloudflare) so we can read and answer you. A hashed form of your IP address is used only to limit abuse, and each message triggers a notification email to us through Scaleway (Transactional Email, France). We use this only to reply and to improve the app. We do not share or sell it, and you can ask us to delete it. When you open the message screen, the app asks the support server for your earlier conversation with us, using your installation identifier and a private key stored on your device; no other personal data is sent at that moment.

Stored on your device only

Your favourite icons, the light or dark mode you choose, which offers you dismissed, and your installation identifier are stored on your device. The app checks on your device whether other Mortek icon packs are installed, to show the right offers; this result is not sent anywhere. Rate this app, the developer page and the other packs open Google Play or a web page in your browser, which has its own privacy policy. We receive nothing from those links.

Permissions

The only permission is INTERNET, used when you tap Send on an icon request or a message, and when you open the message screen to load your earlier conversation. The app does not use the advertising id, your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Deletion

To have your requests, messages and email address deleted, write to the address below. Deleting a request or message removes the stored thread from our server. It does not automatically remove the notification emails already sent to the developer mailbox, or the rate limit records, which hold only a hashed form of your IP address and a counter. Email us to have the notification emails deleted. Everything else (the pack itself) is removed from your device when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or Switzerland), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we hold is what you send with an icon request or message; to use these rights, contact us by email.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
