Privacy Policy: Crystal Garden: Idle Merge

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Crystal Garden: Idle Merge (package com.mortek.crystalgarden) collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

We do not sell any personal data, and we collect none except what you send in the optional support chat (see below). The game has no account and no login. Your progress is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the game. Your save may be included in your device's Google backup if you have it enabled.

Advertising

The current version of the game shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics software. If we add optional rewarded ads in a later version, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent before any ad is requested.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases, such as gems, packs, Auto merge and Remove Ads, are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which products you own and a purchase reference from Google Play, so it can unlock them and avoid crediting a purchase twice. These are stored only on your device, in the game's save file. Tapping Restore purchases asks Google Play which one-time products your account owns.

Links to other games and pages

The Settings screen has a "More games" list, a Rate this app button and links to this policy and the terms. Tapping one opens the Google Play app or a web page in your browser. The game itself sends no data when showing or opening them.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Support chat

The game has an optional Contact support message form in Settings. Only if you send a message, the message text, an optional email address you choose to enter, your language code, app version, platform and a random install identifier (not linked to your identity) are sent to our support server, which runs on Cloudflare. A hashed form of your IP address is used only to limit abuse. Each new message also triggers a notification email to us, sent through Scaleway (Transactional Email, France), so the message text and the email address you may have entered pass through that service. Scaleway acts only as our processor. We use this data only to answer you. We do not share or sell it. You can delete your conversation at any time in the app (Settings, Support), which removes the stored thread and all its messages from our server, or ask us to delete it by email. It does not automatically remove the notification emails already sent to the developer mailbox, or the rate limit records, which hold only a hashed form of your IP address and a counter, no message content, in one-hour windows. Email us to have the notification emails deleted.

Data security and deletion

Data sent to our support server or handled by Google services is encrypted in transit. Local game data is removed when you uninstall the game. Support conversations are stored on our server until you delete them in the app or ask us to. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or Switzerland), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we hold is what you send in the support chat; to use these rights, delete the conversation in the app or contact us by email.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
