Delete your data: Stitched Leather: Icon Pack

The Android icon pack Stitched Leather: Icon Pack (package com.mortek.icons.leather) is published on Google Play by Mortek and has no account. We only hold data if you sent an icon request or a message to the developer from the app. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to mauricemoret1991@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data (Stitched Leather: Icon Pack)". Write from the email address you typed in the app, or tell us the approximate date and the text of your request or message, so we can find it. We do not need any other information.

What we delete

The icon requests and messages you sent (including the list of app package names), the email address you entered, and the random installation identifier linked to them. We also delete the notification emails about your request from our mailbox. Rate limit records only hold a hashed form of your IP address and a counter and expire on their own.

When

We confirm by email and delete within 30 days, usually within a week.
